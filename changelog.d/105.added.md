- `benchmark@0.1.0`, a research agent that compares candidate libraries for one need on fixed criteria (licence, maintenance, documented performance, size, language support). Every cell of its table is a `Claim`, so a number can appear only with a word-for-word citation, and "not published" is preferred to a guess. It recommends a candidate and says what the choice depends on, or names a `spike` when the decision hinges on a number nobody has published. Candidates that are not given are found with `web.search`; its eval cases name their candidates and are graded by rubric and `citations_resolve` (#105).
