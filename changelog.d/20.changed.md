- `factory review` now acts on diff-triage's decision. A confident skip runs no reviewer, judge gate or judge and ships with exit 0, the document saying `"outcome": "skipped"` and the judge gate status `triage_skipped`; a route that names a proper subset of the panel runs only those reviewers. A route of `all`, an empty or unknown route, an escalation, a Jev error or a truncated diff still runs every reviewer, and `--no-triage` still runs exactly the named reviewers (#20).
- A permanent 5% sample of the runs that would skip (`SKIP_SAMPLE_RATE` in `pipelines/review_panel.py`) still gets the full panel, so a drift in the skip gate shows up as a finding on a sampled run. `scripts/shadow_report.py` marks those runs `(sampled)`, lists them under `"sampled"` in its JSON, and `--fail-on` applies to them; a triage-skipped run reports as `skipped`, not as unchecked (#20).
- `JevSpec` gains `act_on_decision`, default `false`, which replaces the `runtime.gates.ROUTE_ONLY` constant. `diff-triage@0.4.0` sets it, and `run_panel` acts only when its caller passes `act_on_triage=True`, which only `factory review` does: the fix, build and hotfix loops log triage and run every reviewer. The `triage:decision` row and the `triage` section of the review document gain `may_act`, `sampled` and `outcome` next to `route_only`. The skip comment says no reviewer looked and how to force one with `--no-triage` (#20).
- The review panel pins `diff-triage@^0.4`. Until `factory publish-check diff-triage@^0.4` has passed, `factory review` exits 3 unless given `--allow-unpublished` or `--no-triage` (#20).
