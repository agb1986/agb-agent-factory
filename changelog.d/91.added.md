- `story-breakdown@0.1`, an agent that turns a PRD into ordered tickets in the `Ticket` shape, each with a size, the indexes of the tickets it depends on and its impacted paths from `deps.impact`. The output model rejects a cycle, a forward or out-of-range dependency and a malformed path, and `StoryOutput.parallel_conflicts()` lists the unordered tickets that share a path (#91).
- A `*` in a rubric case's `paths_exist` walks every item of a list, such as `tickets.*.impacted_paths` (#91).
- The `storefront` and `tracker` fixtures, two small packages with import graphs, and four story-breakdown eval cases on them (#91).
